## Who I Am

I am an Executive Strategy, Business Operations, and Analytics professional with experience supporting growth, performance management, executive decision-making, and operational excellence across fintech, digital financial services, media, and public sector organisations.

My career sits at the intersection of strategy, technology, analytics, and business execution. I work with executive teams to transform complex operational, financial, and customer data into actionable recommendations that improve performance, reduce risk, and accelerate growth.

My background combines a **Bachelor of Technology in Applied Statistics** with a **Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence**, providing a unique blend of quantitative rigor, commercial thinking, and emerging technology expertise.

---

## Strategic Impact

### Fintech & Financial Services

Throughout my tenure at Autochek Africa and Autochek Financial Services, I supported business growth, lending operations, executive reporting, and enterprise performance management across multiple African markets.

Key achievements include:

* Improved enterprise reporting accuracy by **95%** through implementation of governance controls and quality assurance frameworks.
* Reduced reporting turnaround times by **70%** through process automation and dashboard modernization.
* Reduced business-critical data quality issues by **35%** through governance and operational controls.
* Built executive dashboards tracking lending performance, transaction volumes, portfolio quality, customer behavior, revenue growth, and regulatory KPIs.
* Supported market expansion analysis, portfolio performance reviews, and strategic planning initiatives across multiple African markets.
* Developed customer churn and revenue forecasting models that informed business planning and investment prioritisation.
* Contributed to board and executive reporting by translating operational and financial performance into actionable business insights.

---

### Media & Commercial Analytics

At Royal Media Services, East Africa's largest media organisation, I support executive leadership through strategic analytics, audience intelligence, and commercial performance reporting.

Key achievements include:

* Established leadership reporting frameworks supporting executive reviews and strategic planning cycles.
* Developed audience segmentation models contributing to a **22% increase in audience engagement**.
* Supported initiatives that contributed to a **15% increase in digital advertising revenue** through improved audience targeting and content optimisation.
* Produced board-level reporting packs covering audience growth, monetisation performance, and content return on investment.
* Led cross-functional analytics initiatives spanning editorial, commercial, digital, and technology teams.
* Delivered strategic recommendations used by senior leadership and major commercial partners.

---

### Public Sector & Healthcare Analytics

At the National Hospital Insurance Fund (NHIF), I supported national healthcare performance monitoring and policy readiness initiatives.

Key achievements include:

* Built automated reporting dashboards for enrolment trends, claims analysis, and regional performance monitoring.
* Supported Universal Healthcare planning through structured reporting and capacity assessments.
* Enabled data-driven policy readiness reviews through improved performance visibility and operational reporting.
