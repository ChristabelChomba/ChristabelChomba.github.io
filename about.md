## Who I Am

I don't think the biggest threat to a good programme, product, or public system is bad intentions — it's small, boring failures that nobody was watching for. A record that didn't get entered. A migration that wasn't checked. A dashboard nobody QA'd before a decision got made off it. An intervention nobody tested before it was scaled. Those failures compound quietly until the person on the other end doesn't get what they were promised.

My work sits in that gap. I've spent the last five years making sure data is entered correctly, cleaned properly, modelled honestly, governed deliberately, and tested rigorously — across a national health insurer, a pan-African fintech, and a media house that runs research for NGOs, government, and businesses across East Africa.

My background combines a **Bachelor of Technology in Applied Statistics** with a **Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence** — the statistical rigor to know when evidence is actually strong enough to act on, and the technical grounding to build the systems that produce it.

## How I Think About Impact

I evaluate my own work the way I'd want any intervention evaluated: not by how much got built, but by what changed for the people depending on it.

<div class="impact-framework">

  <div class="impact-pillar">
    <h4>Scale</h4>
    <p>How many people, records, decisions, or markets actually touch this system? A regional health dataset and a single dashboard are not the same bet.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="impact-pillar">
    <h4>Evidence & Rigor</h4>
    <p>Is the claim backed by a randomised trial, a QA process, an audit trail — or by a good story? I try to only make the claims the evidence supports.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="impact-pillar">
    <h4>Neglected Work</h4>
    <p>Record management, data cleaning, migration QA, access controls — unglamorous work that almost nobody wants to own, and where most real failures actually happen.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="impact-pillar">
    <h4>Follow-Through</h4>
    <p>Did the report change a decision? Did the flagged gap get fixed? Work that stops at "delivered" isn't finished — it's parked.</p>
  </div>

</div>
